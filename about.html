Our Journey

Two Decades of Growth

From a single workshop to a multi-city service network — here's how ApplianceHub became India's trusted appliance partner.

2004
Humble Beginnings
Founded as a small refrigerator repair shop in Chennai with 3 technicians and a dream to deliver reliable service.
2008
First Showroom Opened
Opened our first appliance retail showroom, becoming an authorized dealer for Samsung and LG in Tamil Nadu.
2013
ISO Certification
Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality management systems — a first for an independent appliance dealer in the region.
2018
Multi-City Expansion
Expanded to Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Coimbatore with 5 showrooms and a fleet of 40+ service vehicles.
2024
Digital Service Platform
Launched our online booking platform and mobile app, enabling same-day service booking for 15+ cities across South India.
